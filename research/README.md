# KAN-Flow-X Research

Research exploration of KAN-based hierarchical flow matching for multi-task robotic manipulation.

## Status: Architecture Designed, First-Pass Results In

- **Actual performance**: ~26-30% on MT-50 (19.4M trainable params, 1 seed)
- **Baseline context**: Behavior cloning on MT-50 typically lands 40-60% with proper training
- **What works**: Solves ~3 trivial tasks (drawer-close, window-open, window-close)
- **What doesn't**: Fails on grasping (pick-place, peg-insert) and precise reaching

## Honest Positioning

This is an exploration of whether KAN-based architectures with hierarchical flow matching and task routing can improve multi-task robotic manipulation. The first-pass results (26-30% MT-50) are below typical behavior cloning baselines, suggesting either:
1. The architecture needs more training/hyperparameter tuning
2. The 19.4M trainable parameter budget is insufficient for MT-50
3. The component combination needs rethinking

Ablation studies will isolate which components help and which don't.
